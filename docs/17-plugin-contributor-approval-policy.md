# Developer Contribution, Publication, and Compensation Policy — Proposal

**Draft only.** This page does not constitute a binding contributor agreement, a promise of payment, or an operational marketplace.

## Separate decisions

1. **GitHub repository contribution accepted:** public files may be merged after maintainer review. They are not necessarily executable or supported.
2. **Product compatibility approved:** a particular immutable release, license, security findings and capabilities have been separately assessed.
3. **Marketplace listing approved:** platform owner authorizes the description, attribution, support expectations, compatibility, policy disclosures and release version.
4. **Individual business installation approved:** authorized tenant administrator consents to the exact permissions for that tenant.
5. **Developer compensation approved:** separate mutually accepted contract sets the model and payment conditions.

No earlier decision implies a later one.

## Contributor expectations

Contributors may fork the public repository, open proposals and submit independent code with explicit authorship and a usable software license. They do not need or receive private application source. A contributor must not submit copied proprietary code, personal information, customer data, secrets, malware, undisclosed telemetry, or unexpected remote execution. Document network connections, external processing, package dependencies, data retention, and support responsibilities.

Reviewers may reject or request changes at any time; publication is never automatic. Existing releases can be suspended, delisted, or revoked when justified by safety, legal, security, quality or compatibility problems.

## Free-to-customer design direction

The proposed Hubzam Works™ marketplace offers approved extensions to customers without a **separate per-plugin purchase charge**. Developer compensation, if offered, would come through a separate Hubzam Works™ program that might be fixed, usage-based, or hybrid. Do not announce rates or guarantees until they are approved and supported by formal terms. Normal platform subscriptions and third-party service charges must be explained separately.

## Public catalog status labels

Use **Proposed**, **In Community Review**, **Source Accepted**, **Product Review**, **Marketplace Approved**, **Suspended** and **Archived** only when supported by actual records. `Source Accepted` must never be presented as `Marketplace Approved`.

## Intellectual property

The Hubzam Works™ brand and proprietary core remain privately owned. Contributors retain rights to their independent work unless they agree otherwise in writing, and must have rights to grant the licenses needed for distribution. Documentation and examples do not grant rights to the private application or trademarks. Formal agreements should be reviewed by qualified counsel before launching financial incentives.
